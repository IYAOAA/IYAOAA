How to Troubleshoot a Printer That Won’t Print

Posted • 2025 • by Ibrahim

Printers are great — until they suddenly refuse to do their one job: print. Before you throw it out the window, take a breath. Most printer issues are simple to fix once you know where to look.

This guide walks you through the most common causes and quick ways to get your printer working again.

🖨️ Step 1: Check the Basics

  • Make sure the printer is plugged in and turned on.
  • Check your USB/Wi-Fi connection — for USB, try another port; for Wi-Fi, ensure it’s on the same network.
  • Inspect for paper jams or low ink/toner warnings.

⚙️ Step 2: Set the Right Printer as Default

On Windows 10/11:

  • Go to Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners.
  • Click your printer → Set as default.

On macOS:

  • Go to System Settings → Printers & Scanners.
  • Select your printer → Set as default.

🧾 Step 3: Clear the Print Queue

On Windows:

  • Open Control Panel → Devices and Printers.
  • Right-click your printer → See what’s printing → Cancel All Documents.

On macOS:

  • Go to System Settings → Printers & Scanners → Open Print Queue.
  • Delete or pause stuck jobs.

🔁 Step 4: Restart Everything

Restart your printer, computer, and Wi-Fi router. Give it a minute to reconnect before sending another job.

🧩 Step 5: Update or Reinstall Printer Drivers

On Windows:

  • Open Device Manager → Printers → Right-click your printer → Update driver.
  • Or visit your manufacturer’s website (HP, Canon, Epson, etc.).

On macOS:

  • Remove and re-add the printer under Printers & Scanners.

📶 Step 6: Check Network Connection (Wireless Printers)

Use the printer’s built-in screen or app to reconnect to Wi-Fi. Assign a static IP to prevent it disappearing after router restarts.

🔧 Step 7: Run the Built-in Troubleshooter (Windows)

  • Go to Settings → System → Troubleshoot → Other troubleshooters → Printer.
  • Run it to auto-detect and fix common issues.

✅ Step 8: Test on Another Device

If it prints from another device, your PC settings are the issue — not the printer itself.

🧠 Final Thoughts

Printer issues usually come down to simple things: wrong default, outdated drivers, or jammed queues. Before assuming the worst, restart, reconnect, and retry. It often works like magic.
